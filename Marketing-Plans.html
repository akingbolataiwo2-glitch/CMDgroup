How it works

From free review to monthly report

Six steps

  1. Free review: we look at your store and show where sales are being lost.
  2. Quote: we confirm the plan, add-ons and monthly price in writing.
  3. Onboarding: week 1, we get access and set up tracking and a baseline.
  4. First 30 days: fixes with the most impact first, usually product pages, tracking and abandoned cart email.
  5. Monthly report and call: what we did, what moved, what is next.
  6. Quarterly review: keep, upgrade or pause the plan based on the numbers.

What we need from you

  • Shopify store access (staff or collaborator invite)
  • Access to Google Analytics and Search Console
  • Access to your email platform and ad account, if you have them
  • Brand assets: logo, colours, product images
  • One person who replies within two working days

Terms: a 3 month minimum, then month to month with 30 days' written notice. You pay Meta and TikTok directly from your own ad account, and you own your ad accounts, store, domain and content from day one. Payment goes through Upwork or Fiverr for now, billed monthly in advance. The first review or tip is free; extra work is quoted first. We do not guarantee sales or rankings. We promise the work, the monthly report and the monthly call.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. The automated audit and report are free. Fixing what it finds is separate work that we quote before starting.

Does it work on any website?

No. It is built for Shopify stores only. If your site is on another platform we tell you so instead of guessing.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two minutes. You can leave the page; we email your report link when it is ready.

What do you look at?

Only your public storefront and Shopify's public endpoints, the same things a shopper or search engine can see. We never log in to your store.

Can you see my sales or traffic?

No. Traffic and sales need third-party or store-connected data, so the report marks them as unavailable instead of inventing numbers.

What happens to my details?

We use your name and email to send the report and follow up about it. We do not sell them. See our Privacy Policy.
